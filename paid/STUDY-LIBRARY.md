# Independent study library

Revision: October 16, 2026. The library contains 30 original lessons, roughly 19,000 words including prompts and metadata, spanning all 98 objective identifiers used by the existing CIP 2023 question bank. Structural coverage is verified automatically; it does not establish official endorsement, independent subject review or empirical exam readiness.

## Learner experience

- Dashboard → Open study library; filter by domain or search lesson titles, summaries and objective identifiers.
- Results → Read domain notes or Read the related study lesson for an individual question.
- Dashboard recommendations link missed objective identifiers directly to the related lesson.
- Each lesson has three highlighted essentials, three explanatory sections, a decision sequence, an original worked example, common mistakes, and a concealed ungraded recall answer. Each now also includes two deeper explanatory sections, a four-row concept comparison, a challenge scenario with decision reasoning and alternatives, and two additional self-checks with concealed reasoning.
- Practice this lesson starts up to ten eligible questions mapped to that lesson's objectives. It preserves the reserved fifth mock and refuses to silently replace a different active study focus.
- Notes require a verified account where verification is enabled and active paid or trial entitlement. Timed simulations block notes within the service until submitted or expired. This does not police use of external materials.
- No new personal-data fields, reading telemetry, third-party scripts or email notifications are introduced. Optional read-aloud uses browser/device speech; online voices may process lesson text through the device provider, as explained in the controls and privacy notice. Reading is not counted as assessed mastery.

## Editorial method

The teaching prose and examples were written for this product. AIIM's public blueprint supplied alignment identifiers and domain boundaries, not copied lessons or proprietary course text. Highlighted essentials are editorial priorities; no knowledge of live questions is claimed. The app retains its current exam-version notice. Examples of retention periods and calculation methods are instructional and do not establish an organization's legal duties.

## Updating safely

Edit `study-content.mjs` and the original extensions in `study-depth.mjs`, maintain stable lesson identifiers, and update the version/date when meaning changes. Keep every bank objective mapped exactly once; tests check unique mapping, eligible practice, basic depth and access boundaries. Add a new lesson or split a group when editorial review identifies missing teaching depth. Do not claim psychometric validation from these checks. Reassess alignment before supporting the revised exam.

The content module is server-only; API authorization protects runtime access. The public source repository remains a separate distribution issue pending the owner's explicit repository-privacy decision.

## Optional lesson read-aloud

Listen to lesson starts only after an explicit action. Pause cancels the current short passage; Resume restarts that passage, while completed passages are preserved. Stop and leaving the lesson cancel reading. The full lesson includes the original and deeper teaching content, worked scenarios and self-check questions, but not hidden self-check answers. Essentials-only mode omits deeper content. Voice and speed options are in Reading options and help. On-device English voices are preferred; device defaults or online voices may process text through a provider. We request no microphone access and store no listening activity.

Keyboard controls use native buttons/selects; headings, table captions and row/column headers support assistive technology. Unsupported browsers show an explicit fallback to the student’s screen reader or device reading feature. These checks do not establish complete accessibility conformance or audibility on every device.
